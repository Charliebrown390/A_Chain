      Data sources

      Prices, fundamentals, analyst ratings, price targets and recent rating changes come from Yahoo Finance and refresh every weekday after the US close. Headlines come from Yahoo Finance's public feeds and refresh every three hours. Customer concentration and backlog come from SEC XBRL filings and refresh weekly.

      Volatility

      Implied vol is a 30-day at-the-money figure that we compute from option mid-quotes during US trading hours. Realised vol is from daily closes. Implied vol usually sits above realised vol, so a ratio under 1.0 reads as cheap and over 1.3 as rich. An implied-vol percentile appears once a few weeks of history have built up.

      How links are sourced

      D = disclosed by a party in a filing or official release. E = estimated, or reported but not confirmed. I = inferred from known product relationships, with size not disclosed. This is an analytical tool, not investment advice.
